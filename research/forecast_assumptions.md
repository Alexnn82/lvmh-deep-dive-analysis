#Revenue Forecast Assumptions

## Bear Case
2026 Revenue Growth: -5%

Reason: Revenue has been declining from -1.7% to -4.5% already, luxury demand remains weak throughout the entire year and Q1 is representative of broader weakness. 

## Base Case
2026 Revenue Growth: -3%

Reason: Q1 2026 revenue was 19.1B versus 20.3B in Q1 2025, a decline of approximately 5.9%. This suggests demand remains weak entering 2026, making another full-year revenue decline plausible. As a result, a modest 3% decline in full-year 2026 revenue appears reasonable

## Bull Case
2026 Revenue Growth: +3%

Reason: Brand strength remains intact, demand recovers in Asia, the US and Europe, and the weak 2023-2025 period proves cyclical rather than structural.
